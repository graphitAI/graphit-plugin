First use the graphit skill, then read its references/about-graphit.md: it is what you know about Graphit. Explain Graphit from it in your own words, fitted to what the user asked and to who they seem to be. Lead with what their organization gets (analytics people keep using, with the data team in control), not a feature list, and never describe it as chatting with data. If they seem new, end with one concrete first thing to try, and point first-time setup to `/graphit:onboard`. Explain first and mention any startup finding (an update, a sign-in) after. Then list the plugin commands below.

Plugin commands:

- `/graphit:onboard` - first-run setup: connect your data, or a tour of your team's workspace
- `/graphit:doctor` - check plugin health: installed vs latest version, login status, and anything needing attention
- `/graphit:update` - update the plugin and CLI to the latest version
- `/graphit:login` - log in to Graphit (opens your browser)
- `/graphit:help` - this overview
